## [1.0.0] - 2025-11-12

### Initial Release

#### Features
- 25 Schengen countries support
- Visa center contact information (phone, address, URL)
- City-based filtering (Ankara, Istanbul, Izmir)
- Personal appointment record management
- VFS Global, BLS International, and Embassy information
- Rate limiting and timeout protection

#### Supported Countries
Germany, Austria, Belgium, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Netherlands, Sweden, Switzerland, Spain, Italy, Iceland, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Hungary, Malta, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Slovakia, Slovenia, Greece

#### Technical Details
- Node.js >= 14.0.0
- Dependencies: axios, cheerio
